The app is a single-page Streamlit application, migrated from an earlier multi-page Dash build — a deliberate simplification that collapsed a multi-callback Dash app into one reactive script that's far easier to reason about and deploy. The architecture keeps two concerns strictly apart: data_processing.py owns every dataset and the exposure model, while streamlit_app.py is pure presentation, so the analytics can be tested and reused independently of the UI.

Because loading five datasets and merging 112 GeoJSON files on every interaction would make the app crawl, the expensive work is wrapped in Streamlit's @st.cache_data — the full dashboard bundle is computed once and reused across reruns, and an uploaded timeline is parsed once and cached against its bytes. Every map click, week change, and slider move then re-renders instantly off the cached data. The whole thing is deployed on Streamlit Community Cloud with a Procfile fallback for any host, and ships with a pytest smoke-test suite.
